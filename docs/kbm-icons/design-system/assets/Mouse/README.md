Mouse glyphs, 20×27 at 1080p: a body in `prompt-ink` (#f9f9f9) with a 1.5px outline and pressed zone in `glyph-legend` (#000000). Both inks are fixed in the SVG, so they render the same on every surface.

- `mouse-left.svg`: left button, the right trigger (RT).
- `mouse-right.svg`: right button, the left trigger (LT).
- `mouse-middle.svg`: middle button, the right stick click.
- `mouse-wheel-up.svg`: wheel up, the right bumper (RB).
- `mouse-wheel-down.svg`: wheel down, the left bumper (LB).
- `mouse-move.svg`: movement, the right stick (camera).
